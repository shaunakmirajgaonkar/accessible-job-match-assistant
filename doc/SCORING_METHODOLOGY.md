# Scoring Methodology

The Accessible Job-Match Assistant uses a transparent weighted scoring workflow.

- Skill overlap — 38%
- Accessibility requirement coverage — 32%
- Commute fit — 12%
- Work-mode fit — 6%
- Salary fit — 4%
- Workplace support — 8%

The final result is normalized to a 0–100 match score.

The score is a decision-support signal, not an automated hiring decision, disability assessment, or guarantee of job suitability.
